# Commercial and White-Label Information

GeoPulse Studio 5.1.0 is an MIT-licensed Next.js application for website crawler-access audits, content editing, structured article hosting, agency reports, and configured API publishing. The source is available for technical evaluation and commercial use under the license terms.

## Evaluation scope

Agencies and software teams can assess:

- Configurable agency branding and signed shareable client reports with browser print-to-PDF.
- Bilingual content editing and heuristic scoring across configurable engine profiles.
- Website crawler-rule observations, structured article pages, a sitemap, and `/llms.txt`.
- Authorized publishing integrations, delivery records, publishing credits, plans, and redemption codes.
- A topic queue that creates editorial drafts and requires human review before scheduled external distribution.

Operators need to configure their own domain, credentials, payment integrations, branding, policies, backups, and account access. External publishing and AI providers retain their own terms, availability, and charges. The application uses embedded SQLite and still requires a compatible Node runtime and persistent storage.

## Due diligence

Run the repository's current tests and review the deployment, authorization boundaries, secret management, payment reconciliation, data retention, and recovery procedures in the intended environment. Test counts and passing results should come from the checked-out revision and its test output.

Content scores measure application heuristics. Neither those scores, structured data, crawler access, nor cross-platform publication establish actual AI citations, ranking improvements, revenue, or customer acquisition. Unsupported competitor pricing, acquisition examples, valuation figures, fixed delivery times, and customer-result claims are excluded from this description.

Payment ingress verifies live, settled LemonSqueezy order receipts against an explicit variant-to-SKU mapping, or a generic shared-secret bridge keyed by monetary payment receipt. Overlapping native subscription notifications are ignored; native recurring renewal, refund/reversal handling, and Stripe signature verification are not implemented. A trusted bridge must verify provider events, product prices, settlement, and live mode before forwarding normalized renewal payments. The optional growth pack creates private evidence research drafts and does not publish promotional articles.

Any proposed commercial transfer, service engagement, or licensing arrangement requires a separate written agreement covering the specific assets, third-party components, permissions, and support. This repository does not establish exclusive IP ownership or a valuation.
