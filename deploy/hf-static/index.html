This Hugging Face Space is a static showcase. The application links open the production website; server features and workspace data are hosted there.

01 / OBSERVE

Check crawler access

Inspect publicly accessible crawler rules, readable HTML, structured data, and llms.txt. Each audit describes observations at request time.

02 / REVIEW

Build an editorial workflow

Edit bilingual content with heuristic quality suggestions. Eligible verified tutorials can pass the publication gate; unsupported research needs evidence and editor review.

03 / PUBLISH

Use configured channels

Maintain public article pages and submit content through authorized platform integrations. Track recorded results and inspect partial failures. Explore the article hub ↗

Review before outreach

Record recipient permission evidence, review the recipient and full message, and explicitly approve the current draft. An administrator submits approved versions. Provider acceptance does not establish delivery or human readership.

Understand the observations

Crawler access does not prove crawling, indexing, ranking, or AI citation. Scores and priorities are heuristics. Scheduled queue runs are attempts, and do not guarantee daily publication or business outcomes.
